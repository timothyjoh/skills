# Title of the video

Every build in this lineup makes a video from this brief, so the outputs compare side by side. Use only the facts below. Do not invent features, numbers, quotes, customers or logos.

## The video

- Subject: what the video is about, in one or two sentences.
- Audience: who watches it, and what they already know.
- Length: 30 to 45 seconds.
- Format: 16:9, 1920x1080, 24 fps or higher, H.264 video with AAC audio, in an `.mp4` file.
- Sound: narration, music, or both, as the skill does by default. Silence is allowed only if the skill cannot make sound; say so in the build log.
- End card: the URL or line the video ends on.

## Facts you may use

1. One fact per line, each one true and checkable.
2. Name real commands, features and limits exactly.

## Real material you may show

Commands, code, output blocks or screenshot paths that are real and safe to show on screen.

## Sources

Public pages:

- https://example.com/the-real-page

Read-only local sources (read them; never edit them):

- /absolute/path/to/a/local/repo

Demo URL for a running web app (argo records it): none

## Suggested story (follow your skill's own structure if it has one)

1. Hook: the problem in one line.
2. The answer: what it is and the one action it takes.
3. How it works: one or two concrete beats.
4. Why trust it: one proof point from the facts.
5. End card.
